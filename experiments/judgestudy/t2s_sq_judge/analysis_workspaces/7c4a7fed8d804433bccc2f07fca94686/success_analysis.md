# Success Memory Item 1
## Title
Direct Match Extraction for Trivia Queries
## Description
Prioritize exact or near-exact textual matches in retrieved snippets, particularly from flashcards, quizzes, or study materials, to instantly resolve factoid questions.
## Content
When a query closely mirrors retrieved text, treat the surrounding snippet as a direct answer source. Extract the specific entity or term completing the phrase without additional inference or synthesis, as trivia databases often store questions and answers verbatim together.
