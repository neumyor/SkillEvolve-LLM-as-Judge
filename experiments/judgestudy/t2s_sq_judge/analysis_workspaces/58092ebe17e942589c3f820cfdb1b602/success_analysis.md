# Success Memory Item 1
## Title
Leverage Prior Knowledge with Lexical Alignment for Trivia Queries
## Description
When answering trivia or quiz-style questions that lack detailed explanatory context, combine established domain knowledge with direct keyword matching in the retrieved documents to derive and ground the answer.
## Content
Trivia prompts often appear as isolated statements or repeated phrases in search results without providing full narratives. In these scenarios, anchor your reasoning in strong factual associations related to the prompt's core concepts. Simultaneously, scan the retrieved snippets for explicit lexical matches or direct mentions that align with your initial hypothesis. This approach efficiently bridges gaps in sparse or repetitive documentation, ensuring the final answer is both logically sound and textually supported before formatting.
