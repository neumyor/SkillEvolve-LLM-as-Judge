# Success Memory Item 1
## Title
Align Prompt Years with Institutional Milestones
## Description
Match specific years in trivia questions to historical events like founding, reactivation, or relocation mentioned in context, rather than assuming literal founding dates.
## Content
When a question specifies a year tied to a hall or organization, scan context for exact matches or closely related milestones. Distinguish between initial creation and subsequent pivotal dates that may be referenced in trivia sources, ensuring the timeline aligns with the prompt's constraint.

# Success Memory Item 2
## Title
Identify Parent Organization from Hall References
## Description
Recognize when a question asks for the governing body or parent organization behind a hall of fame, using context to link the hall to its sponsoring entity.
## Content
If a query references a hall of fame by its members or attributes, determine whether the expected answer is the hall itself or the overarching organization. Extract the parent entity from context sentences that describe who established, manages, or houses the hall.

# Success Memory Item 3
## Title
Cross-Reference Dates to Eliminate Alternatives
## Description
Use explicit dates and founding information in context to rule out similarly named institutions and confirm the correct target.
## Content
Compare the target year in the prompt against known dates for candidate organizations or halls. Actively discard options with mismatched founding dates or operational histories, relying on contextual evidence to narrow down to the single valid entity.
