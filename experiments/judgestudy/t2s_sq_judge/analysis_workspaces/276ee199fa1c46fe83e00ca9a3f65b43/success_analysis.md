# Success Memory Item 1
## Title
Extract Acronym Expansions via Title-Text Alignment
## Description
Use document titles that pair a shortened term with a longer phrase to resolve what an acronym or nickname stands for, especially when body text is incomplete.
## Content
When a query asks for the full form of an abbreviation or coined term, scan retrieved document titles for the pattern "[Term]: [Full Phrase]". Titles frequently serve as authoritative expansions even when paragraph snippets are truncated or lack explicit definitional sentences. Cross-reference the title match with any passing references in the body text to confirm alignment before finalizing the answer.

# Success Memory Item 2
## Title
Reconstruct Truncated Definitions Using Structural Cues
## Description
Infer missing parts of a definition when snippets cut off after keywords like "short," "abbr.," or "stands for" by leveraging surrounding metadata and contextual phrasing.
## Content
Search results often truncate explanatory passages mid-sentence. When encountering terms followed by ellipses or cutoffs immediately after indicators like "short" or "stands for," treat the preceding document title, header, or adjacent phrases as the likely completion. Prioritize structural consistency over guessing, and verify that the inferred expansion logically fits the subject matter described in the surrounding snippets.

# Success Memory Item 3
## Title
Anchor Queries to Direct Snippet Matches Before Extraction
## Description
Locate distinctive phrases from the prompt within retrieved text to identify the correct source document, then extract the target information from that specific context block.
## Content
Prompts often contain unique quotes, idioms, or specific phrasing that appear verbatim in search snippets. Match these phrases first to isolate the most relevant document. Once anchored, shift focus to that document’s title, headers, or immediate surrounding text to find definitions, expansions, or factual answers, avoiding distraction from unrelated results that may share superficial keywords.
